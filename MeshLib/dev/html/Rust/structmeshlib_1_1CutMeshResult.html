◆ fbs_with_contour_intersections()

auto meshlib::CutMeshResult::fbs_with_contour_intersections ( ) const -> &meshlib::FaceBitSet

Bitset of bad triangles - triangles where input contours have intersections and cannot be cut and filled in a good way; under force fill, also the triangles where two segments of the contours cross (segments that follow each other along a contour or have a common mesh vertex do not), even if their cut is valid; the triangles where that cannot be told may be reported too: where the exact predicates fail on the points of the contours that they compare (e.g. coincident contours of a double-sided sheet), or, without sortData, where the corners of the triangle are on one line and more than three points of the contours are inside it
