Detailed Description

a torus as a mathematical object: the surface swept by a circle of radius minorRadius, whose center moves around the axis on the circle of radius majorRadius Generated from class MR::Torus3d.

Generated from class MR::Torus3d. Heap-allocated instances are held in Owned<Torus3d>.
