#include <MRMesh/MRRigidXfRansac.h>

Finds the rigid transformation of source points to target points from given matches, many of which can be wrong: tests the transformations through random samples of three matches and keeps the one with most inliers, then refines it by least squares fitting on all its inliers.

Parameters
matches(source point, target point) pairs, e.g. from findFPFHMatches(...)
Returns
rigid transformation of source points to target points, or error if there are less than three matches, no consistent sample was found or progress returned false
