Detailed Description

template<int nBits>
class MR::FastInt< nBits >

signed integer of nBits bits, which must be a multiple of 64 and at least 256 (below that use FastInt128 and Int64Mul128); the product of two of them is exact, because it is twice as wide as the arguments; as FastInt128 it lacks conversion in double, sqrt-function and stream input/output
