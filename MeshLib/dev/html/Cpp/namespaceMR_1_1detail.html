◆ getPointDegrees()

template<int N = cMaxPerturbedPoints, bool Bounded = false>
MR_FORCE_INLINE int MR::detail::getPointDegrees ( const PreciseVertCoords * vs,
const std::int64_t * radices,
int n,
PointDegree * res,
std::int64_t * places = nullptr )

finds the degrees of the perturbation of points vs[0..n-1] and writes them in res[0..n-1]: sorted by ids, the points get the degrees 1, r_0, r_0 * r_1, ..., where r_k is the radix of the k-th id, given in radices[] of every point with that id, and points with equal ids get equal degrees; the smallest id moves most, as in orient3d, and the radix of a point must exceed its weight in every term of the polynomials of the predicate (the sum of 9, 3, 1 over its coordinates in the term), so that the degrees order the terms as the perturbation of orient3d does: by the weights of the points from the largest id down; returns the number of different ids, and writes their degrees in ascending order in places[] if it is not null; n is at most N; the caller makes the degrees fit in int64, unless Bounded: then only the first ids whose terms fit count, since the terms with the k-th id and smaller ones have degrees below r_0 * ... * r_k, and the points of the other ids get the degree 0, which leaves them unperturbed at every stage (see PerturbationStagesOf)

the degrees of the terms with this id do not fit: the ids before it count, and the points of the others stay unperturbed

skip to support triangles with shared vertices

std::size_t and not int, to be deducible from std::array

the exact product of two two's-complement values given by their 64-bit words, which always fits in the sum of their word counts; the only multiplication of this file

schoolbook multiplication of unsigned values

this row adds nothing: 0 * b[j] leaves every res[i + j] unchanged, and res[i + m] is still 0 (never written before this row), so the skipped res[i + m] = carry (carry stays 0 here) would be a no-op. Small-magnitude values keep their high words at 0, so this is the common fast path; negative operands are sign-extended to all-ones top words and are not skipped.

at most ( 2^64 - 1 )^2 + 2 * ( 2^64 - 1 ) < 2^128 here

never written before, since the loop above stops at i + m - 1

a negative argument was taken 2^(64*words) times larger than it is; both subtractions below end exactly at the end of res, so the borrow never leaves it

◆ orient3dPoly()

template<std::int64_t M>
VolumePoly< M > MR::detail::orient3dPoly ( const PointDegree & a,
const PointDegree & b,
const PointDegree & c,
const PointDegree & d )
nodiscard

the volume of tetrahedron abcd (see orient3dVolume) of the perturbed points as a polynomial of eps, without the terms of degrees above M

every element of the matrix with the rows (a-d), (b-d), (c-d) is a polynomial: delta + eps^degP - eps^degD, whose terms are stored below (skipping zero delta and the terms of unperturbed points), the last term is -eps^degD if d is perturbed

the determinant is the sum over 6 permutations of the signed products of three elements, each product has up to 27 monomials; the product of the last terms of three elements (eps^(13*degD) with the sign of permutation) is excluded, since 6 of them cancel one another

all coefficients are not larger than 2^32 by absolute value, so the product of two of them fits in int64

◆ cMaxPolyDTriTri

std::int64_t MR::detail::cMaxPolyDTriTri = 15'943'959
constexpr

the largest degrees of the polynomial terms that segmentIntersectionOrder and segmentIntersectionTriPlaneOrder consider first, found experimentally (the leading term of nom, or the leading term of at least one of two orient3d-polynomials for the segment's ends) in the tests and in a random sweep of degenerate inputs; the terms of higher degrees are not stored to save computation time, and if they are not enough to decide, the predicates repeat the computation with all terms (cMaxPolyDAll); the tests count the inputs above them, so they live here rather than in MRPrecisePredicates3.cpp
