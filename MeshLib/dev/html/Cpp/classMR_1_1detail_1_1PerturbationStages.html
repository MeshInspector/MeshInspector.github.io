Detailed Description

segmentIntersectionTriPlaneOrder: it reaches the polynomial path with all three largest ids in the plane

the points of a predicate with the degrees of their perturbation, which moves them in stages: at stage s = 1, ..., numStages() only the points with the s smallest ids are perturbed (orient3dPoly leaves the others unperturbed); a polynomial of the predicate that is not zero at stage s already has the leading term of the full one, since every term with a point of a later stage has a larger degree than every term without; and if it was zero at stage s - 1, its terms without the points of stage s cancel one another, so only the products of the terms of degrees from place( s ) up remain (see from in lowestTermOfSumOfProducts)
