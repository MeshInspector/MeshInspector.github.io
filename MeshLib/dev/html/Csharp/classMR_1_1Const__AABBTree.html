Detailed Description

bounding volume hierarchy of mesh triangles with up to 4 children per node and up to 4 triangles per leaf; the children of a node are stored contiguously, and every leaf keeps the vertices and the boxes of its triangles Generated from class MR.AABBTree. This is the const half of the class.
