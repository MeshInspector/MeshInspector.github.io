◆ locators

unsafe MR.Std.Const_Vector_MRFeaturesAlignPair MR.Features.Const_AlignParams.locators
getprotected set

Their centers are matched in the least squares sense by the motion the primary pair leaves free: spin about the primary direction and, for planes, translation along them. For a point primary, the first locator sets the direction to spin about. A locator's lever arm (its distance from the primary axis, or for planes from the locator centroid) should be well above its fitting noise, otherwise the noise sets the spin.
