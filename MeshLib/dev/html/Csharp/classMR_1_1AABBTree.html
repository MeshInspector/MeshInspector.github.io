Detailed Description

bounding volume hierarchy of mesh triangles; the leaves store the vertices of their triangles, and the boxes of nodes and of leaves are kept in one array boxes(), so a change of mesh topology requires either Mesh.invalidateCaches() or updateLeafVerts(), and MeshTopology.flipOrientation() requires flipOrientation() here (as Mesh.flipOrientation() does) Generated from class MR.AABBTree. Base classes: Direct: (non-virtual) MR::AABBTreeBase&lt;MR::AABBTreeTraits&lt;MR::FaceTag, MR.Box3f&gt;&gt; This is the non-const half of the class.

Parameters
