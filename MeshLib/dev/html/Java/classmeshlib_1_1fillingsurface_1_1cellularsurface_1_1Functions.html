Estimate the width that is needed to attain the targetDensity. Inverse of estimateDensity.

Note
The width is not unique in general, no guarantees are made about which value among possible will be returned. Due to the simplification of the formula (sphere must either fully contain the intersection of cylinders or be inside it), solution not always exists.
