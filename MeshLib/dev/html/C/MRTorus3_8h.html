typedef struct MR_Torus3f MR_Torus3f

a torus as a mathematical object: the surface swept by a circle of radius minorRadius, whose center moves around the axis on the circle of radius majorRadius Generated from class MR::Torus3f. Supported MR_PassBy modes: MR_PassBy_DefaultConstruct, MR_PassBy_Copy, MR_PassBy_Move (and MR_PassBy_DefaultArgument and MR_PassBy_NoObject if supported by the callee).
