enum SelfIntersections::Settings_Method

Fix method

A C++ enum: the constants are numbers, so this also holds values that aren't named constants.

Enumerator
Relax 

Relax mesh around self-intersections

CutAndFill 

Cut and re-fill regions around self-intersections (may fall back to Relax)

SelfBoolean 

Cut the mesh along the exact contours of its self-intersections (see selfBoolean) and keep only the largest by area part of every connected component; colliding components get united, and the holes that the contours reach are closed for the cut. The cut is made once and can leave degenerate faces and tiny self-intersections: call fixMeshDegeneracies after it, and repeat both if needed. Of the other settings only callback and new2Old are used
