Detailed Description

bounding volume hierarchy of mesh triangles with up to 4 children per node and up to 4 triangles per leaf in SIMD layout: every internal node keeps the boxes of its children, and every leaf the boxes of its triangles; the triangles of leaf l are in slots [4 * l, 4 * l + 4) of leafTris(), its unused slots have invalid faces; a node is referenced as in AABBTreeSimdNode::child, or by NodeId: the internal nodes first, then the leaves Generated from class MR::AABBTree.

Instances created here own their C++ objects, see CppObject. new X(ffi.RAW, pointer, owned, owner) wraps an existing object.
