Detailed Description

bounding volume hierarchy of a point cloud with up to 4 children per node in SIMD layout: every internal node keeps the boxes of its children; the points of leaf l are orderedPoints() [MaxNumPointsInLeaf * l, MaxNumPointsInLeaf * ( l + 1 ) ), all leaves are full except the last one
