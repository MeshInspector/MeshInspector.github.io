Detailed Description

bounding volume hierarchy of mesh triangles with up to 4 children per node and up to 4 triangles per leaf; the children of a node are stored contiguously, and every leaf keeps the vertices and the boxes of its triangles Generated from class MR::AABBTree.

Generated from class MR::AABBTree. Heap-allocated instances are held in Owned<AABBTree>.
