float mrmeshpy.ConstDistanceMeasurementObject.computeDistance ( self) 
Computes the distance value. This is affected by `getDistanceMode()`.
In `euclidean`, this is `getWorldDelta().length() * (isNegative() ? -1 : 1)`.
