constructs topology from given triangles in parallel, if every edge has at most two triangles with opposite directions on it,
and the triangles around every vertex form either one closed fan or at most two open fans; returns false and leaves this and settings.region unchanged otherwise;
triangles with invalid or repeated vertices are not added, as in MeshBuilder::fromTriangles with the same settings;
\\param nonManifoldVertsOnly if given, set to whether it failed only on vertices having several fans of triangles around, but not just one or two open fans

◆ collapseEdge()

EdgeId mrmeshpy.MeshTopology.collapseEdge ( self,
ConstEdgeId e,
Const_func_void_from_Id_EdgeTag_Id_EdgeTag onEdgeDel ) 
collapses given edge in a vertex and deletes
1) faces: left( e ) and right( e );
2) edges: e, next( e.sym() ), prev( e.sym() ), and optionally next( e ), prev( e ) if their left and right triangles are deleted;
3) all vertices that lost their last edge;
calls onEdgeDel for every deleted edge (del) including given (e);
if valid (rem) is given then dest( del ) = dest( rem ) and their origins are in different ends of collapsing edge, (rem) shall take the place of (del)
\\return prev( e ) if it is still valid
