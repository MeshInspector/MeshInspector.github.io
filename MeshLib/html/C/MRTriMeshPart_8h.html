typedef struct MR_TriMeshPart MR_TriMeshPart

TriMesh with a region of triangles: only the triangles of the region and their vertices are valid Generated from class MR::TriMeshPart. Supported MR_PassBy modes: MR_PassBy_Copy, MR_PassBy_Move (and MR_PassBy_DefaultArgument and MR_PassBy_NoObject if supported by the callee).
